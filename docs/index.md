# SMB Drop

**Send, back up and open files on your NAS or PC share from any Android app.**
No cloud, no accounts, no ads, no tracking — your files go only to your own server.

- [Source code and screenshots on GitHub](https://github.com/tonybenoy/smbdrop)
- [Download the latest APK](https://github.com/tonybenoy/smbdrop/releases/latest)
- [Privacy policy](privacy.html)
- [Report a problem or ask a question](https://github.com/tonybenoy/smbdrop/issues)
