SMB Drop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

SMB Drop is an Android app that sends files from your phone to an SMB (Windows/NAS/Samba) file share that you own or are allowed to use, and lets you browse and open files on that share.

What we collect
Nothing. SMB Drop has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer does not receive any data from the app.

What the app stores on your phone
• The servers you add (address, share name, folder, user name and the encrypted password). Passwords are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore and never leave your phone except to sign in to your own server.
• Your settings, routing rules, bookmarks, the upload queue and a list of files already uploaded (to avoid duplicates).
• Temporary copies of files you share into the app, kept only until they are uploaded.
• Cached thumbnails of photos on your server (limited to 200 MB, removable from Android settings → Storage).

Where your data goes
Files and credentials are sent only to the server address you enter, over your local network or the connection you configure (SMB 2/3). The app does not contact any other server.

Permissions
• Network / Wi-Fi state: connect to your server and decide when to upload.
• Photos and videos (media): back up your camera folder to your server, if you turn backup on.
• Location (optional): Android requires it to read the Wi-Fi network name, used only for the "back up only on these Wi-Fi networks" option. Your location is not collected or stored.
• Notifications: show upload progress and errors.
• Foreground service (data sync): keep uploads running while the screen is off.
• Biometrics (optional): lock the app.

Deleting your data
Remove a server in Settings → Destinations, or clear the app's storage / uninstall the app. Files already on your server are under your control and are not affected.

Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Contact
Questions about this policy: see the contact address on the app's Google Play listing.
